## E. In-process vs C zlib at level 6

In-process flate2 (miniz_oxide, Cargo.lock:149-155) deflates 14.1 MB in the 10.85 s
delta = ~13 MB/s. C zlib via `gzip -6` does 9.3 MB/s. The Rust backend is NOT slower
than zlib here, so a backend swap has no measured support at level 6.
